Signs Your Battery Needs Replacing

Lithium batteries degrade gradually, and most riders don't notice until the decline is severe. Watch for these signs:

  • Noticeably shorter range — if a ride that used to leave you at 40% battery now drains to 10%, the pack's capacity has likely degraded.
  • Voltage sag under load — power that dips or stutters on hills or acceleration, even with a full charge shown.
  • Faster self-discharge — losing significant charge just sitting unused for a few days.
  • Physical swelling — a pack that looks puffed up or won't sit flush anymore should be retired immediately, not just monitored.
  • Charging time changes — a pack that used to take 4 hours and now takes 6+ for the same charge is showing internal resistance increase, a classic aging sign.

Most e-bike batteries are rated for 500-1000 full charge cycles before capacity drops meaningfully — depending on riding habits, that's typically 3-5 years for a daily commuter.
