How to Read Your Charger Label

Every e-bike or scooter charger has a small label — usually on the underside or on a sticker near the cord — printed with two key numbers: an input rating and an output rating.

The output line is what matters for battery matching. It looks something like "Output: 54.6V ⎇ 2A". That 54.6V is not your battery's nominal voltage — it's the fully-charged peak voltage, which is always higher than the nominal rating.

Here's the quick conversion table:

  • 42V charger → 36V battery family
  • 54.6V charger → 48V battery family
  • 58.8V or 59.9V charger → 52V battery family
  • 84V charger → 72V battery family

If you've lost your battery's documentation, checking the charger label is the fastest way to confirm which voltage family you're actually on — and it's more reliable than guessing from your bike's marketing name, since manufacturers don't always put the voltage in the model name.
