Connector Types Explained

Voltage matching gets most of the attention, but connector shape matters just as much — a battery can be the exact right voltage and still be physically or electrically incompatible if the connector doesn't match.

Common connector types in e-bikes and scooters:

  • XT60 / XT90 — Common bullet-style connectors on many aftermarket and hobbyist battery packs.
  • Anderson (Powerpole) — Often used on higher-power e-bike systems and some scooters.
  • 5.5mm x 2.1mm barrel — Extremely common on charger ports specifically (not the battery-to-controller connection), used across many different voltage families — which is exactly why voltage must be double-checked separately.
  • Proprietary manufacturer connectors — Many major brands (Rad Power, Aventon, Bosch, Shimano systems) use their own connector shapes specifically to prevent third-party swaps, for better or worse.

Before buying any replacement, compare a clear photo of your existing connector's pin count and shape against the listing photos — not just the voltage spec.
