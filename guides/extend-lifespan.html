Extending Your Battery's Lifespan

Lithium batteries degrade from chemistry, not just age — and a few habits make a measurable difference over years of use:

  • Avoid charging to 100% every time. If your battery or app allows it, charging to 80-90% for daily use and reserving full charges for long rides reduces stress on the cells.
  • Don't let it sit empty. Storing a battery at 0% for extended periods can push cells into a damaging deep-discharge state.
  • Store around 40-60% charge if you won't ride for more than a few weeks — this is the ideal resting state for lithium chemistry.
  • Avoid extreme temperatures. Both charging in freezing cold and storing in direct summer heat accelerate degradation.
  • Use the charger that came with it, or a verified same-voltage replacement — mismatched charge curves add unnecessary stress.
