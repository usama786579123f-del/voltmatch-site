Third-Party vs OEM Battery Packs

An OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer) battery is made or officially sold by your bike's brand. A third-party battery is made by an unrelated company but designed to match the same voltage and physical mounting.

When a third-party pack is usually fine: when it matches your exact voltage family, uses the same connector type, and comes from a seller with genuine reviews from owners of your specific model — not just generic "compatible with most e-bikes" marketing.

When to stick with OEM: if your bike is still under warranty (third-party batteries often void it), if you can't find clear connector photos to compare, or if the seller can't tell you the exact cell configuration (like "13S5P" or "10S4P").

Price alone isn't a reliable signal — a cheap OEM-brand replacement can be a safer bet than an unbranded pack, even if the unbranded one costs less.
