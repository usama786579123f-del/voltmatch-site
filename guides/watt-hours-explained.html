Understanding Watt-Hours and Range

When comparing batteries, Ah (amp-hours) alone is misleading because it ignores voltage. Two batteries can both say "14Ah" and have very different actual energy storage if one is 36V and the other is 48V.

The number that actually tells you total energy is Watt-hours (Wh), calculated simply as:

Wh = Voltage × Ah

For example, a 48V 14Ah battery stores 672Wh — noticeably more than a 36V 14Ah battery, which only stores 504Wh, despite the identical Ah rating.

When shopping for range, always compare Wh, not Ah. A higher-voltage battery with the same Ah number will always store more usable energy, translating to more real-world range.
