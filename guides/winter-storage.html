Storing Your Battery in Winter

Cold weather doesn't just reduce range temporarily — improper winter storage can permanently shorten a lithium battery's lifespan.

Key rules for winter storage:

  • Bring the battery indoors if temperatures drop below freezing — never leave it on the bike in an unheated garage or outdoors overnight.
  • Charge to roughly 40-60% before long-term storage, not 100%.
  • Never charge a battery that is still cold from being outside — let it reach room temperature first, since charging cold cells can cause internal lithium plating that permanently reduces capacity.
  • Check the charge level roughly once a month during storage, topping up if it's dropped below 20-30%.

Riders who store batteries properly through winter typically see noticeably better range retention the following spring compared to those who leave batteries in cold garages at full or empty charge.
