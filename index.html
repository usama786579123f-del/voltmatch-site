Battery 101

Voltage (V) is the electrical pressure your battery sends to the motor. Most e-bikes and scooters use 36V, 48V, or 52V systems.

Capacity (Ah) is how much charge the battery holds. Energy (Wh) = Voltage × Ah, and is the best way to compare range across different batteries.

Charger output is always slightly higher than nominal voltage — a 48V battery charges at 54.6V, a 36V battery at 42V. This is how you can verify a battery's real family even without the spec sheet.
